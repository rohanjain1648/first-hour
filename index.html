A mixed-reality crisis drill

First Hour

A flood hits a small town on your table. Your four AI department heads disagree, whisper behind your back, and act when you look away. You have one hour, ten real minutes, and two hands.

How to play
  • Sit at a table. Everything is a pinch: grab tokens from the tray and drop them on the town.
  • Pinch an agent's gold badge for a report; pinch ✓ or ✗ on their proposals.
  • Pinch the bead on a glowing thread to overhear a private whisper. They will notice.
  • Hand a gold chip to an agent to let them act without asking.
  • Optional shortcuts: thumb-tap your index finger to approve, swipe left to deny.
  • No headset? You get an emulated Quest in a virtual room. Drag with the right mouse button to look around; switch the emulator to hands and hold the left button to pinch.
AI, privacy and content
  • Agent dialogue is generated by AI (Anthropic's Claude) or by built-in scripts. It may be imperfect.
  • Harrow Bend, its people and its agencies are fictional. This is a training game, not emergency advice.
  • No account, no personal data. Progress is saved only in this browser.
  • Voice commands use your browser's speech service only while you hold the mic on; audio is never stored by First Hour.
  • Depicts a flood emergency without injury imagery. Suitable for ages 10+.

Hands only. Seated. Works on Meta Quest and Meta VR Glasses.
